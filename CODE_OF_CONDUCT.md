# Code of conduct

Dura POS is built for and with people who run small businesses, many of whom
are new to software projects. Everyone is welcome here, whatever their
experience, language, background or identity.

## What we expect

- Be patient and kind, especially with first-time contributors and shop
  owners asking for help.
- Assume good intent. Ask before concluding.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Prefer plain language to jargon. Not everyone reads English as a first
  language.
- Respect privacy. Never share someone's business records, contact details or
  credentials.

## What isn't acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes or language.
- Personal or political attacks.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Pressuring anyone to pay for help that the project offers for free.
- Anything else a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately to the maintainer, through the private contact
described in [SECURITY](SECURITY.md). It applies to conduct as well as
vulnerabilities. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove
content, or restrict or ban participation, in proportion to the harm.

This code applies in all project spaces, and wherever someone represents the
project. It's inspired by the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
